How fairness works

① Players on court are excluded from the queue. ② Available players are ranked by fewest completed games, then highest waiting score. ③ Four eligible players are selected for doubles. ④ Teams are split to minimize the difference between team-average skill ratings, then favor partners who have played together less often; exact ties are randomized. ⑤ Enter scores before finishing. ⑥ Finishing updates wins, losses, points, rankings, queue balance, and match history.
