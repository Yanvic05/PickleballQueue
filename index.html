How fairness works

① Players on court are excluded. ② Fair rotation ranks by fewest games, then wait score. ③ Skill-balanced mode selects four from the next eight eligible players, favoring a tighter skill range while still considering queue position. ④ Matching mode controls team formation: Balanced, Social Mix, Skill Separated, or Winners / Losers. Balanced avoids a four-player lineup with exactly one 3.0 rating whenever an alternative exists. ⑤ Enter scores before finishing. ⑥ Finishing updates wins, losses, points, rankings, queue balance, and match history.
